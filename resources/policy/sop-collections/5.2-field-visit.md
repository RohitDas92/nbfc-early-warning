A field visit is an in-person visit to the borrower's recorded residential
address by an authorised employee or agent.

A field visit requires **forty-five days past due or more**. It requires that
contact with the co-applicant under section 3.4 has been attempted first. It is
authorised by a manager.

**A field visit is forbidden while the account is in moratorium.**

The forty-five day threshold is deliberately higher than the thirty-day threshold
for handover. A visit is the most intrusive step available before legal recovery
and is justified only where remote contact has been given a full further cycle to
work.

A visit may be made only to the residential address recorded on the loan. Visits
to a workplace, an educational institution, or the address of any person who is
not a party to the loan are prohibited under section 4.2.

The visit is subject to the hours in section 4.1. The agent must identify
themselves and the company at the outset, must leave immediately if asked to, and
must not enter the property without invitation. No cash may be collected and no
identity document may be retained or photographed.

The visit must be logged whether or not the borrower was met, with the date, the
address attended, the agent's name, and the outcome. A visit that produced no
contact is still evidence that the step was taken.
