Handover transfers day-to-day management of a delinquent account from the
servicing analyst to the specialist collections team.

Handover requires **thirty days past due or more**. It requires that soft contact
with the borrower under section 3.1 and contact with the co-applicant under
section 3.4 have both been attempted first. It is authorised by a manager.

**Handover is forbidden while the account is in moratorium**, without exception.
An account in moratorium is not delinquent in the sense this section addresses,
and an amount which is not yet contractually due cannot be in arrears.

The thirty-day threshold aligns handover with entry into SMA-1 under section 2.1.
An account that has crossed thirty days has missed a full cycle and has not
responded to two attempts at contact.

Both preconditions must be evidenced in the contact history. An attempt that was
made but not logged does not satisfy the precondition, and handover must not be
approved on an assurance that contact took place.

Handover changes who manages the account. It does not by itself authorise any
further action. A field visit still requires section 5.2 to be satisfied on its
own terms, and a settlement still requires the Settlement Policy to be satisfied
on its.
