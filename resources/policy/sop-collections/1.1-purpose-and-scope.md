This Standard Operating Procedure governs the collections function for the
education loan portfolio. It applies to every account from the first day of
delinquency until the account is cured, settled, written off or transferred to
legal recovery.

The SOP has three jobs. It states which action may be taken at which stage of
delinquency. It states who may authorise each action. And it states what may
never be done, regardless of who asks.

The SOP is binding on employees of the company and on any third party acting on
its behalf, including outsourced recovery agents and tele-calling vendors. Under
RBI/2022-23/108 paragraph 1, the ultimate responsibility for outsourced activity
rests with the regulated entity. Engaging an agency does not transfer that
responsibility, and a breach by an agent is treated as a breach by the company.

Where this SOP is silent, staff must escalate rather than improvise. Where this
SOP conflicts with an RBI direction, the RBI direction prevails and the conflict
must be reported to the Head of Collections within one working day.

Nothing in this SOP authorises an action that the account's approval matrix
forbids. The action policy table is the operative control; this document explains
it. Where the two disagree, the table denies the action and the disagreement is a
defect to be fixed, not a judgement call to be made by the analyst.
