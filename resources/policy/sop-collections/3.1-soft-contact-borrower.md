Soft contact is a reminder communication to the borrower by telephone, SMS,
email or letter, for the purpose of informing them of an amount due or overdue
and arranging payment.

Soft contact is permitted from zero days past due. It requires no prior action
and no approval beyond the analyst handling the account.

Soft contact is the normal first step and is a precondition for several later
actions. Contact with a co-applicant under section 3.4 requires that soft contact
with the borrower has been attempted first. Handover to collections under section
5.1 requires both.

Every attempt must be logged in the contact history with the date, the channel
and the outcome, whether or not the borrower answered. A contact attempt that is
not logged has, for the purposes of this SOP, not occurred, and cannot be relied
on to satisfy a precondition.

All soft contact is subject without exception to the conduct rules in section 4,
including permitted hours. A reminder call placed at seven-thirty in the evening
is a breach whatever its content and however courteous its tone.

Soft contact does not include any communication that states or implies a
consequence which has not been approved under this SOP. Staff must not warn a
borrower of a field visit, a handover, a settlement offer or a legal step unless
that action has actually been authorised for the account.
