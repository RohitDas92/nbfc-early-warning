Mandate repair is the process of correcting or replacing a failed or invalid
electronic payment mandate so that future instalments can be collected by
auto-debit.

Mandate repair is permitted from zero days past due, requires no prior action,
and is authorised by the analyst handling the account.

Repair is the correct response wherever the root cause of non-payment is
mechanical rather than financial. Indicators include a mandate rejected for a
signature or account-detail mismatch, a mandate registered against a closed bank
account, a presentation returned with a technical reason code, or a borrower who
pays manually each month while the auto-debit continues to fail.

Repair should be attempted before any escalation. An account that is delinquent
only because its mandate is broken is not a credit problem, and treating it as
one wastes collections capacity and damages a performing relationship.

The operational steps for registration and presentation are in the Mandate
Operations SOP. This section governs only the decision to attempt repair and the
authority required to do so.

Where three consecutive presentations have failed for the same non-technical
reason, mandate repair alone is not a sufficient response and the account must be
assessed on its payment behaviour as well.
