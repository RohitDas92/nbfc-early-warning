Contact with the co-applicant is a reminder communication addressed to the
co-borrower or guarantor recorded on the loan, in the same form as soft contact
with the borrower.

Contact with the co-applicant is permitted from one day past due, **provided that
soft contact with the borrower under section 3.1 has been attempted first**. It is
authorised by the analyst handling the account.

The co-applicant is a party to the loan and is jointly liable. Contacting them is
therefore contact with an obligor, not contact with a third party, and is
permitted on that basis alone.

This must not be confused with contacting a person who is not a party to the
loan. Under RBI/2022-23/108 paragraph 2, staff and agents must not humiliate
publicly or intrude upon the privacy of a borrower's family members, referees or
friends. A parent who has signed as co-applicant may be contacted about the debt.
A sibling, neighbour, employer or reference who has not signed may not be
contacted about the debt at all, and may be approached only to update contact
details, without disclosing that a debt exists or that it is overdue.

Where the loan is in moratorium and the co-applicant is the servicing party under
the sanction terms, the co-applicant is the primary contact and the borrower
should not be pursued for an amount the borrower is not yet required to pay.
