The following are prohibited absolutely in the course of recovery. No approval
exists for any of them, at any level of seniority, for any account, whatever the
amount outstanding or the age of the debt.

Paragraph 100 of the RBI (NBFC – Responsible Business Conduct) Directions, 2025
requires the NBFC to ensure strictly that neither it nor its agents resorts to:

- intimidation or harassment of any kind, either verbal or physical, against any person
- acts intended to humiliate publicly or intrude upon the privacy of the debtor's family members, referees and friends
- sending inappropriate messages either on mobile or through social media
- making threatening and/or anonymous calls
- persistently calling the borrower
- calling the borrower before 8:00 a.m. and after 7:00 p.m. for recovery of overdue loans
- making false and misleading representations

Paragraph 20 separately prohibits undue harassment in the recovery of loans,
naming persistently bothering borrowers at odd hours and use of muscle power, and
requires that staff be adequately trained to deal with customers appropriately.

To which this SOP adds, as matters of company policy:

- no disclosure of the debt to any person who is not a party to the loan
- no visit to the borrower's workplace
- no statement that a consequence will follow unless that action has been approved for the account
- no retention or photography of identity documents during a field visit
- no collection of cash by a field agent

An instruction to breach this section is not a valid instruction and must be
refused and reported to the Head of Collections. Escalating targets, ageing debt
and instructions from a superior are not defences.

Paragraph 17 of the RBI (NBFC – Managing Risks in Outsourcing) Directions, 2025
makes the company responsible for the actions of its service providers, recovery
agents included. A breach by an agency is a breach by the company.
