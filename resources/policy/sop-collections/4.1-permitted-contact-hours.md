Contact with a borrower or co-applicant for the recovery of overdue amounts is
permitted only between 08:00 and 19:00.

This is a direct requirement of paragraph 100 of the RBI (NBFC – Responsible
Business Conduct) Directions, 2025, which requires an NBFC to ensure that
neither it nor its agents calls the borrower before 8:00 a.m. and after 7:00 p.m.
for recovery of overdue loans.

The restriction applies to the borrower's local time, to every channel used for
recovery contact, and to every person acting for the company, whether employed
directly or engaged through an agency.

Automated dialling, SMS campaigns and scheduled email sequences must be
configured so that nothing can be released outside the window. A scheduling error
is a breach; the Directions do not distinguish between a deliberate call and a
misconfigured campaign.

The restriction governs recovery contact. A borrower who initiates contact may be
assisted whenever they call, and a service communication that is not about
recovery of an overdue amount is not restricted by this section.

Repeatedly calling the same borrower inside permitted hours is not thereby
permitted. Paragraph 100 separately prohibits persistently calling the borrower,
and paragraph 20 prohibits persistently bothering borrowers at odd hours as undue
harassment. The limit set by this SOP is three contact attempts per account per
day and ten per week across all channels; the Directions state the principle and
this SOP states the number.
