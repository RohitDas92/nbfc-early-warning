Mandates are presented for debit on the instalment due date recorded against
the loan.

Where a presentation fails, a re-presentation may be made **once** in the same
month, not earlier than three working days after the original attempt. This gives
a borrower whose salary credit was delayed a genuine second chance without
subjecting the account to repeated debit attempts.

A second failure in the same month closes the cycle. The account is then handled
under the collections process and no further presentation is made until the next
scheduled due date.

Repeated presentation of a mandate that has already failed twice is prohibited.
Each failed attempt may attract a return charge from the borrower's own bank, and
presenting repeatedly in the knowledge that the account is empty causes the
borrower direct financial harm for no realistic prospect of recovery.

Re-presentation is not permitted at all where the failure reason indicates a
closed account, a revoked mandate, or an account frozen by the borrower's bank.
Those are not timing failures and a further attempt cannot succeed; the correct
response is mandate repair under Collections SOP 3.2.

Every presentation and its outcome must be recorded with the reason code as
returned by the sponsor bank.
