Legal recovery covers action taken through a court, tribunal or arbitral
forum to recover a debt that ordinary collections has not resolved.

An account is accepted into legal recovery only where it is more than one
hundred and eighty days past due, has been handed over to the collections team
under Collections SOP 5.1, and at least one field visit has been attempted under
Collections SOP 5.2 or the borrower is confirmed untraceable.

Acceptance is a decision of the Legal team, not a transfer the collections team
can make unilaterally. A file that does not meet the three conditions is returned
with reasons.

On acceptance, all collections activity on the account stops. Parallel pursuit by
two teams produces inconsistent statements to the borrower, and an inconsistent
statement is a gift to opposing counsel.

The Legal team does not re-open the question of whether the debt is owed. It
takes the outstanding position as recorded and tests whether it is recoverable
through legal process. Where the file shows a dispute about the amount itself,
the account is returned to collections for reconciliation first.

This SOP is restricted to the Legal department. Collections staff may be told
that an account has been accepted for legal recovery; they may not be told what
strategy is being pursued.
