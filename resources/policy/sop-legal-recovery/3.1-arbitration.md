Where the loan agreement contains an arbitration clause, arbitration is the
default route for a monetary claim and is invoked in preference to a civil suit.

Invocation requires the written approval of the Head of Legal. The notice
invoking arbitration is issued only after the demand notice under section 2.1 has
expired unanswered or been answered unsatisfactorily.

The arbitrator must be appointed strictly in the manner the agreement provides.
A unilateral appointment by the company, where the clause does not clearly permit
it, is liable to be set aside and will cost more time than it saves.

The claim as pleaded must match the ledger. Any difference between the amount
claimed in arbitration and the amount in the demand notice must be explained on
the file before filing, not afterwards under cross-examination.

Settlement during arbitration follows the Settlement Policy without modification.
An arbitration in progress does not create authority to waive principal, and does
not move a waiver into a lower approval slab.

This section is restricted to the Legal department. The strategy, the choice of
forum and the company's assessment of its own position are privileged and must
not be disclosed to collections staff, to outsourced agents, or to the borrower.
