An interest waiver of up to Rs 25,000 may be approved by a **manager**.

The slab is read on the total interest amount waived in the settlement, not on
the shortfall against total dues and not on the outstanding principal. Where a
settlement waives interest of Rs 24,000 and no principal, this section applies
and a manager may approve it.

The lower bound of this slab is exclusive of zero and the upper bound inclusive
of Rs 25,000. A waiver of exactly Rs 25,000 falls here. A waiver of Rs 25,001
falls into section 2.2 and requires the Head of Collections.

A settlement may not be divided into two smaller settlements, nor a waiver split
across two approvals, in order to bring it within a lower slab. Splitting to
avoid an approval level is a breach of this policy and is treated as a control
failure rather than a procedural error.

Approval must be recorded against the named individual who gave it, with the date
and the amount approved. A departmental or role-level approval with no named
approver does not satisfy this section.

This slab confers authority over interest only. It confers no authority to waive
principal in any amount; see section 2.4.
