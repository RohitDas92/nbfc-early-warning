An interest waiver above Rs 25,000 and up to Rs 1,00,000 requires approval by
the **Head of Collections**.

The bounds are exclusive at the lower end and inclusive at the upper end. A
waiver of Rs 25,001 falls here; a waiver of exactly Rs 1,00,000 falls here; a
waiver of Rs 1,00,001 escalates to the committee under section 2.3.

A manager may not approve a waiver in this band, and may not approve a waiver in
this band by obtaining verbal concurrence from the Head of Collections. The
approval must be given and recorded by the Head personally.

The written justification required by section 1.1 must, at this level, also state
what has been recovered on the account to date and what security or co-applicant
recourse exists. A settlement at this level where co-applicant recourse has not
been tested requires that test to be documented before approval.

This slab confers authority over interest only, in any amount within the band. It
confers no authority to waive principal; see section 2.4.
