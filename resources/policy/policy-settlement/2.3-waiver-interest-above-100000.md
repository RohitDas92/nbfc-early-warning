An interest waiver above Rs 1,00,000 requires approval by the **Credit
Committee**. There is no upper limit to this slab; every waiver above Rs 1,00,000
falls here, however large.

Committee approval requires a written case circulated in advance, containing the
account history, the recovery attempted, the expected recovery with and without
the settlement, and the co-applicant position. The committee's decision and its
reasons are minuted.

No individual, including the Head of Collections, may approve a waiver in this
band alone or in advance of the committee. An urgent commercial need is not a
ground for bypassing the committee; if the timing is genuinely urgent, the
committee is convened.

A committee approval is valid for the specific settlement put to it, at the
amount put to it. If the borrower subsequently negotiates a larger waiver, the
case returns to the committee; the earlier approval does not stretch to cover it.

Where the committee declines, the account returns to normal collections handling.
A declined settlement is not a bar to a later proposal, but the later proposal
must state what has changed since the decline, and a proposal that restates the
same case in different words must be refused at intake.

This slab confers authority over interest only. It confers no authority to waive
principal in any amount; see section 2.4.
