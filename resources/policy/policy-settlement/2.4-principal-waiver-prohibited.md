**No waiver of principal is permitted on this portfolio, in any amount, at any
level of approval.**

There is no approval slab for principal. This is not an omission and not a
threshold set very high. No person and no committee holds the authority, and the
absence of any authorised approver is itself the prohibition.

A settlement may therefore waive accrued interest within the slabs in sections
2.1 to 2.3, and may waive penal charges, but must recover the principal
outstanding in full.

A proposal that reduces principal is refused at intake and is not escalated for
consideration. Escalating it invites the appearance that a sufficiently senior
approver could grant it, and none can.

Attempts to achieve the same effect by other means are equally prohibited: a
settlement that recovers full principal on paper while waiving an equivalent
amount of interest that was never accrued, or a write-off recorded as a
collection, is a breach of this section and a misstatement of the accounts.

Where a principal shortfall is genuinely unrecoverable, the route is write-off
under the Board-approved write-off policy, with the accounting and disclosure
that entails. It is not a settlement.
